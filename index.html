Wells

State data: CA DWR Online System for Well Completion Reports (OSWCR), queried live. Values are as key-entered from driller reports and may be missing or wrong — verify with the original WCR PDF. Most WCRs are placed at the center of the PLSS section (≈1 sq mi), so many stack on one point.

County data: San Diego County DEHQ water-well permits (SanGIS), unincorporated area, permits opened 1975–Aug 2020. Located at the center of the permitted parcel (more precise than section centroids). Permits carry no depth/yield/level; those come from a matched state WCR. A permit does not guarantee a well was drilled.

County documents: tapping a well lists its scanned permit file / completion report from the DEHQ Environmental Health Document Library (looked up live by permit number; optional APN search). Documents open in the County viewer; older logs are handwritten scans.

County WCR values: depth, method, yield, water level and date read by OCR from typed county completion reports are marked “county WCR (OCR)”. Fluorescent red wells have a county report that could not be read (handwritten, no report found) and no state data — tap and open the report to read it yourself. Grey dashed = area not processed yet.

Duplicates: a county permit and a state WCR are flagged as the same well when permit # or APN match with close dates (Likely), or when they are within 0.75 mi with dates within ~4 months (Possible).

Well sites: sites you tag (customer, phone, notes, photo, GPS, APN) are stored only on this device and never uploaded. The APN lookup sends only the coordinates to the County GIS parcel service (SanGIS Assessor Parcels). Use My sites → Backup to move them to another device. Clearing browser data deletes them.

Imagery: Esri World Imagery. Averages exclude missing, zero, and implausible values; n shows how many wells were used.
