Wells

State data: CA DWR Online System for Well Completion Reports (OSWCR), queried live. Values are as key-entered from driller reports and may be missing or wrong — verify with the original WCR PDF. Most WCRs are placed at the center of the PLSS section (≈1 sq mi), so many stack on one point.

County data: San Diego County DEHQ water-well permits (SanGIS), unincorporated area, permits opened 1975–Aug 2020. Located at the center of the permitted parcel (more precise than section centroids). Permits carry no depth/yield/level; those come from a matched state WCR. A permit does not guarantee a well was drilled.

County documents: tapping a well lists its scanned permit file / completion report from the DEHQ Environmental Health Document Library (looked up live by permit number; optional APN search). Documents open in the County viewer; older logs are handwritten scans.

County WCR values: depth, method, yield, water level and date read by OCR from typed county completion reports are marked “county WCR (OCR)”. Fluorescent red wells have a county report that could not be read (handwritten, no report found) and no state data — tap and open the report to read it yourself. Grey dashed = area not processed yet.

Duplicates: a county permit and a state WCR are flagged as the same well when permit # or APN match with close dates (Likely), or when they are within 0.75 mi with dates within ~4 months (Possible).

Well sites: sites you tag (customer, phone, notes, photo, GPS, APN) are stored only on this device and never uploaded. The APN lookup sends only the coordinates to the County GIS parcel service (SanGIS Assessor Parcels). Use My sites → Backup to move them to another device. Clearing browser data deletes them.

Septic / sewer: for a site (or a tapped parcel) the app lists septic layouts, permits and old septic files from the DEHQ document library (searched by APN first, then by street address) and shows the parcel's sewer/septic designation from the County's screening layer (DPW/DEHQ, data through May 2025 — a guide, not proof). 📏 Setbacks draws 50 / 100 / 150 ft rings around the well pin; mark the tank, seepage pit, leach line, sewer line or tight line to check distances. Only APNs, street numbers/names and coordinates are sent to County / SANDAG servers; marks stay on this device.

Address / APN search: the 🔍 box on the map takes an APN (285-030-06-00, 2850300600 or 8-digit 28503006 — preferred, since addresses change or do not exist yet before a house is built) or a San Diego County street address (add the community or ZIP, e.g. “729 Main St, Ramona”). APNs are looked up in the County parcel layer; addresses in SANDAG's regional address locator and the County parcel situs addresses. Riverside and Imperial County work too: 9-digit APNs (XXX-XXX-XXX) are looked up in the Riverside Assessor layer and the Imperial parcels (state DWR parcel layer), and Imperial addresses (e.g. “600 E Main St, El Centro”) in California's statewide address locator. “Ocotillo” is the Imperial County town (92259); “Ocotillo Wells” is the San Diego County one. The typed text is sent only to those County / State / SANDAG public map services; recent searches are kept only on this device.

Imperial County: the magenta Imperial WCRs (DWR) layer is a snapshot of the state OSWCR well completion reports for Imperial County (Ocotillo / Coyote Wells / Plaster City and the rest of the county). Most are placed at the centre of the PLSS section (dashed hollow pins), so the popup shows DWR's location accuracy; a pin may be ~0.5 mi off. PDFs open in DWR's Box viewer. The same WCRs also appear in the live State search.

Los Angeles County: LA County publishes no public well-permit map layer, so LA wells are state DWR OSWCR records only (no county permits). The purple-ring LA County WCRs layer shows OSWCR records cached in the app area by area (starting with Pomona Valley / Claremont / La Verne / San Dimas / Diamond Bar), so they show without a search and offline; tap a ring for the WCR number, location and APN as entered on the state record and a link to the DWR WCR viewer. The same records are counted in the live State search.

My jobs: ⛏ My jobs on the map imports your own Google Earth export (.kmz / .kml) or the matched .json made from it, and draws each job (lines, setback circles, a pin at the well) with its matched County permit / WCR. The file is read on this device only, kept in this browser's storage and never uploaded; Remove my jobs from this device deletes it.

Septic layout: ✏️ Septic layout draws a septic system on the satellite map — well / proposed well, tank, distribution box, seepage pit, leach / sewer / tight / property lines, house, leach field, reserve area, driveway, text and measurements — with your own colours and legend, 50 / 100 / 150 ft setback rings and warnings, and exports a letter-size plot plan (PNG or PDF) with north arrow, bar scale, legend and title block. Layouts are customer job data: they are kept only on this device and never uploaded (use ⬇ Backup to move one). Parcel lines from APN sends only the APN to the County parcel service (SanGIS); parcel lines are approximate, not a survey.

Imagery: Esri World Imagery. Averages exclude missing, zero, and implausible values; n shows how many wells were used.
